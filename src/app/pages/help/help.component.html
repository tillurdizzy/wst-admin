Help

This dashboard is for Board members and trusted managers. The resident site is separate. Use Owners, Units, and Parking to keep the directory accurate.

How every page works

  • The table is the working list. It shows the important fields for that page.
  • Search and Presets at the top filter that list. They do not change the database by themselves.
  • Click a row. The panel under the table loads that record so you can edit it.
  • Nothing is saved until you use a Save button or toggle a checkbox that saves on its own (Admin, Confirm data, Owner occupied).

Owners

Owners is the legal record: name, mailing address, phone, email, login, and which unit they own.

Presets

  • All owners — every owner row.
  • Admins — owners marked Admin.
  • Unconfirmed — Confirm data is off.
  • No email — no email on the owner row.
  • No Auth uuid — no website login yet.
  • No unit linked — owner is not assigned to a unit. Safe list for cleanup.
  • Offsite owners — mailing street does not contain Chimney or Burdine.
  • Sort by first name / last name / unit — same list, different order.

Bottom panel

1. Owner — first name, last name, street, city/state/zip, phone. Save writes only these fields. First name is required (it can be a business name).

2. Units — units this owner currently holds. Owner occupied means they live in that unit. Toggle Owner occupied and it saves immediately. Assign unit moves that unit to this owner. The previous owner loses the unit. We do not insert a second link row. We only change who is on that unit.

3. Authentication — email and website login for the resident site.

  • If there is no login yet, enter an email and use Save email and create Auth.
  • A login is created with temporary password 123456. The owner should change that on first sign-in.
  • After a login exists, the email field is locked. Changing it here would not change the login. Change a login email in the Supabase dashboard if you must.
  • Admin saves when you check or uncheck it. Admin on the resident site can see every unit and edit records. Give this only to Board members and other people you trust. It is not a casual setting.
  • Confirm data also saves when toggled. It does not mean the record is complete. It means what is there is accurate to the best of our knowledge.

4. Updates — last saved date, who saved it, and whether data is confirmed. Delete is only enabled when this owner has no units. That also tries to remove their Auth login.

We do not overwrite an existing owner to “update” them onto another unit. We insert a new owner row when needed and point the unit at that row. Old rows with no unit stay until you delete them by hand.

Units

Units is about the home: who lives there and which cars are on file. Owner name and mailing address still show and can be edited, but the focus is occupancy.

  • Search accepts unit numbers only.
  • Presets: all units, owner occupied, tenant occupied.
  • The panel shows unit facts (street / mail station, bedrooms, square feet, legal unit number).
  • Assign New Owner creates a new owner and points this unit at them. The old owner is left on any other units they still have.
  • Residents vs Tenants: a tenant is a resident who is not the owner. When the unit is owner occupied the heading says Residents. When it is tenant occupied the heading says Tenants. The owner row in that list cannot be edited there — edit the owner on Owners or with the Owner edit button.
  • Add / edit residents and vehicles with the dialogs. Delete a resident removes that row. Clear a vehicle sets the vehicle fields to empty and keeps the parking space.
  • Green / yellow / red dots are confirmation status: all confirmed, mixed, or none confirmed.

Parking

Parking manages the painted spaces and the cars on them.

  • Space ID is the number painted on the car port. Some painted numbers are used twice, so all IDs have .1 or .2 added (200.1 and 200.2) for accurate identification.
  • Unit is who that space belongs to. Declaration usually matches space 200 to unit 200. Neighbors sometimes trade, so they can differ.
  • 600-series spaces are extras (registered overflow, guest, handicap). They are not a home number.
  • Assign Registered Space (600+) points that extra space at a unit.
  • Swap Spaces (under 600) trades the unit assignment between two Space IDs. The cars stay on the painted spaces.
  • Edit Vehicle Owner and Vehicle Description in the dialogs. Clear vehicle empties make, model, color, tag, and name.
  • Parking Space Map and Parking Form open the PDFs over this page. Use the hand tool to drag a zoomed map.
